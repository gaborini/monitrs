# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want monitrs to be a project where anyone can report a bug, ask a question, or
propose a change without needing to brace themselves first.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

* Assuming the other person is acting in good faith.
* Being specific and technical in disagreement. "This breaks on musl because
  `getauxval` is absent" is useful; "this is obviously wrong" is not.
* Accepting that a maintainer may decline a change that is well-built but out of
  scope, and saying so plainly rather than through implication.
* Remembering that "I don't know" and "I was wrong" are both normal engineering
  statements.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

* Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
* Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission — including
  hostnames, usernames, or command lines pulled from a bug report.
* Persisting in a rejected direction after a maintainer has explained the
  decision.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and code review — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub, to the repository
maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and treated confidentially. We
will not retaliate against anyone for reporting in good faith.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face consequences
determined by other maintainers.

Consequences escalate according to severity and repetition: a private correction,
a warning, a temporary ban from interaction, or a permanent ban.
